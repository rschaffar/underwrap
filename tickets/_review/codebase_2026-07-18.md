### F-002 — Network-first bootstrap can seize full administration

### F-003 — Key rotation can destroy the last usable encrypted-store generation before key custody
- Priority: critical
- Confidence: high
- Categories/perspectives: encrypted lifecycle; recoverability
- Locations: `crates/underwrap-backend/src/admin_api/stores/lifecycle.rs:142-270; docs/admin-ui.md:45-60`
- Evidence: The backend swaps in new ciphertext, deletes the old backup, and only then returns the sole new key. Disconnect, reopen failure, registry-save failure, or lost response can occur after destructive commit.
- Consequence: A store can become permanently unrecoverable although the operator never received a successful response or replacement key.

- Validation evidence: V-003/V-009 cover success only; no rename/reopen/registry/response-loss failpoints exist (V-038).
